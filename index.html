Pro · the headline

Auto progression tells you when to go up

Three workouts at the same weight, mostly hitting the same reps, and Jotlift tells you it is time to go up. That is the whole rule.

01

It shows the count it is working from. You can check it against your own history.

02

It never lowers a weight. A lighter day neither counts towards the three nor resets them.

03

It does not tell you what to lift. The next set comes up greyed, ready to change, and Jotlift defers to whatever you actually log.

The run it counted

Mon 18 Aug 60 kg × 8 Counted, 1 Thu 21 Aug 60 kg × 8 Counted, 2 Mon 25 Aug 60 kg × 7 Skipped Thu 28 Aug 60 kg × 8 Counted, 3

Three counted, so Jotlift speaks up. The 7 was lighter than the rest, so it was skipped: it did not count towards the three, and it did not reset them either.
