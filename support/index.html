Support

Questions, problems or feedback about Jotlift.

Email jotliftapp@gmail.com and we will get back to you, usually within a few days. If you are reporting a problem, it helps to say which device and iOS version you are on, and what you were doing when it happened.

For how each part of the app works, see how Jotlift works.

Do I need an account to use Jotlift?

No. Logging works fully offline and no account is needed. Adding an account backs your data up, so a new phone is not a fresh start.

I bought Jotlift Pro and it is not showing

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. If it still does not appear, email us and tell us the Apple Account you bought it on, and we will sort it out.

How do I cancel Jotlift Pro?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple rather than by us, so they are cancelled in your Apple Account. On your iPhone, open the Settings app, tap your name at the top, tap Subscriptions, then Jotlift. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and you keep Pro until the end of the period you have already paid for.

How do I get my data out?

Open Settings in the app, go to Your data, and choose Export your data. The export is a file you keep.

I am moving to a new phone

Open Settings, go to Your data, and use Move to a new device on the old phone and Moving from another phone on the new one. If you have an account, signing in restores your data instead.

How do I delete my account?

See deleting your account and data. It covers both ways to do it, and exactly what is removed and what is kept.

A suggested weight looks wrong

Jotlift suggests going up after three workouts at the same weight where you mostly hit the same reps, and the suggestion says which weight and which three workouts it counted, so you can check it against your own history. It never suggests going down. If it still looks wrong, email us with the exercise and what you expected, and we will look into it.
